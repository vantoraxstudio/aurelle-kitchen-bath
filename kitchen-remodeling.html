Kitchen Remodeling

The kitchen your home has been asking for.

A kitchen is where routine and hospitality meet. We would plan yours around how you cook, gather and move, then dress it in materials you will enjoy for years.

Layout & Functionality

Start with how you move

Before choosing finishes, we would map the daily rhythm of the room: where food is prepared, where dishes land, where people gather and where they should stay out of the way.

  • Clear working zones for preparation, cooking and cleaning
  • Generous, uninterrupted worktop where you actually need it
  • Circulation that lets two people share the room comfortably

Cabinetry & Storage

Storage planned around what you own

Deep drawers for pots, tall pantry units, shallow cabinets for everyday dishes: inventory first, cabinet sizes second. The result is a kitchen that stays calm because everything has a home.

Countertops, Lighting & Finishes

Surfaces, light and detail working as one

Countertops

Stone, quartz and other surfaces differ in pattern, maintenance and feel. We would compare samples against your cabinetry and daily habits.

Lighting

Layers of ambient, task and accent light make a kitchen more usable by day and more welcoming by evening.

Hardware & fixtures

Handles, taps and finishes are small in scale but central to the room's character, so they are chosen as part of the palette.

Design Inspiration

Modern, transitional and timeless directions

Three illustrative directions to start a conversation. Images are stock photography, not completed AURELLE projects.

Modern

Clean planes, handleless fronts and a decisive contrast, such as a dark island against pale cabinetry.

Transitional

Softer profiles and familiar materials, balancing classic character with a contemporary edit.

Timeless

Natural stone, restrained palettes and enduring proportions that avoid trends tied to one moment.
