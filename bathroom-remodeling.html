Vanities & Storage

Make the morning routine effortless

A vanity is the bathroom's workhorse. Drawer depths, outlet positions, mirror height and lighting all affect how it feels to use.

  • Drawers organised for daily essentials
  • Tall linen storage that frees up counters
  • Mirrors and lighting placed for even, flattering illumination

Shower & Bath Inspiration

Wet areas worth lingering in

Walk-in showers, freestanding baths or a considered combination: the right choice depends on space, habits and who uses the room.

  • Clear, easy-to-clean glass and thoughtful niches
  • Comfortable entry, bench and fixture heights
  • Waterproofing, drainage and ventilation discussed early

Tile, Stone & Finish Coordination

One palette from floor to ceiling

Tile and stone carry the character of a bathroom. We would compare scale, texture and slip resistance, then align fittings and hardware with the same family of tones.

Warm stoneSoft, light-reflecting walls and floors.
Muted sageA gentle, natural accent.
IvoryClean, calm backdrop for fittings.
Deep forestDepth for joinery or a feature wall.

Lighting & Space Planning

Light, layout and the smaller bathroom

Layered lighting shapes mood and clarity: soft ambient light, bright but even task light at the mirror, and gentle accents.

In compact rooms, space-efficient planning matters: wall-hung vanities, pocket or glass doors, recessed storage and large-format surfaces can help the room feel open and easy to use.

Every bathroom is different, so we would test options against your actual measurements before committing.

Bathroom FAQ

Questions about bathroom planning

Consider who uses the room, how often, and how much space you have. Households with children or mobility needs may weigh these differently from a primary suite. Measurements usually settle what is realistic.

Think about slip resistance, grout lines, cleaning routines and how the finish feels underfoot. Large-format surfaces mean fewer joints, but each material has trade-offs worth discussing.

Bathrooms are smaller but more technical: waterproofing, ventilation and fixture positions leave less room for improvisation. The FAQ page compares both in more detail.
